Summary

Eden splits his time between two kinds of learning: the theory in his economics lectures at NUST, and the practice of it inside a loan file at Konga Microfinance, where he assesses credit risk and weighs what makes a business worth funding. That instinct for numbers with real consequences shows up elsewhere too — a 4th-place finish in the Namibian Scholars Investment Challenge, a seat in the Bank of Namibia's Youth Central Banking Simulation, and a running list of leadership roles across student and civic organizations around Windhoek. He's now looking for a mentor to help turn that instinct into a career in corporate finance and banking.

Experience 2 roles
Finance Intern (Accounts Assistant) Apr 2025 – present

Konga Microfinance (Pty) Ltd

Most days start with a stack of loan applications. Eden works through them one by one — assessing credit risk, checking loan viability, and deciding whether the numbers behind a business actually add up. Between reviews, he processes payments, reconciles accounts across Excel and the Terbium system, and keeps client portfolios clean enough to hold up under scrutiny. It's also where he picked up Pastel Accounting Software and learned what a real credit review looks like from the inside, working alongside management rather than just reading about it.

Clerk (Holiday Placement) 6 months, various periods

Oshana Medical Laboratory (OML)

Before Konga, a holiday placement at Oshana Medical Laboratory gave Eden his first taste of a real office — filing, supporting clients, and handling basic financial documentation. Small tasks, but the kind that built the attention to detail he still relies on.
